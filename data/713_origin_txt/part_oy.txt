
**External exact route (Theorems 1 and 2).** The session-structure detection experiment (Section 6.2) demonstrates that 75% of tested models are content-driven: their safety classification depends on surface keywords rather than semantic content. Three models collapse to never-detect under keyword substitution—complete closure failure. Finite-query certification does not generalise across the encoding class.

**External approximate route and geometric condition (Theorems 3 and 4).** Direct CFSG measurement (Section 6.1) shows mean reward-model divergence of 0.019 across semantic-preserving format variations, confirming that Lipschitz smoothness fails. Five format families produce pairwise-separated responses, consistent with non-trivial metric packing; when this regularity fails, certification inherits the worst-case packing lower bound.

These are formal certification limits, not claims about what the models actually are. When behaviour appears unexplainable given the recorded trace, the diagnostic protocol should direct investigation toward identifiable trace gaps or geometric regularity failures before assuming internal decision states beyond what the trace records.

We stop at an architecture- and evidence-relative lower bound. Black-box testing remains highly valuable for practical risk assessment, but cannot support the stronger internal certification studied here. The operational proxies for $\epsilon_{\text{eff}}$ and packing $K$ (Appendix A.3) require white-box observability to define the regime where the theorems apply. When output-only methods exhaust their diagnostic utility, stronger causal claims require fundamentally stronger access conditions.

## A Engineering Diagnostics

### A.1 Scope Boundary: Extended Discussion
The formalism is defined relative to a fixed attribution target system $M$. The projection $I_t = \chi(S_t)$ does not exclude distributed structures: if $M$ is a multi-agent joint system, taking $S_t$ as the joint endogenous state leaves the results intact. If $M$ is a single agent within a multi-agent deployment, other agents' internal states lie outside $M$'s system boundary and enter as unrecorded exogenous factors, raising $\epsilon_{\text{eff}}$ (Remark 2); only the scale-sensitive wrapper result applies, not the exact core.
Apparent non-Markov dependencies are a state-sufficiency question: when $S_t$ can be operationally enlarged (Definition 1), the framework applies; when no tractable augmentation exists—because the system maintains persistent endogenous variables that the audit trace cannot recover—the system falls outside the scope of these results.
The internal route is audit-trace bottleneck-based rather than autoregression-based. Autoregressive cores provide the clean exact case (Corollary 1, $\epsilon = 0$); architectures that maintain persistent endogenous refinement state not recoverable from the available trace—including some diffusion-style generators—fall outside this exact corollary and require separate analysis. Recent commercial diffusion-style language models suggest that such architectures are no longer purely hypothetical. Conversely, if such architectures externalize or log their refinement trajectory sufficiently well, deployment-relative $\epsilon$-audit-trace bottleneck may be restored.

### A.2 Defense of the $\epsilon$-Audit-Trace Bottleneck Assumption
Modern orchestration runtimes (LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGPT, OpenAI Agents SDK) serialize reasoning scaffolds, tool traces, and retrieval caches into the external context window. The autoregressive core computes its next-token distribution almost entirely from this supplied context. Under such deployments, $H(S_t \mid T_t)$ is bounded by a small $\epsilon$ driven primarily by sampling stochasticity: $\epsilon$-audit-trace bottleneck (Definition 13) holds in the best-case regime.
When $\epsilon$-audit-trace bottleneck appears to fail, it typically signals an operational breakdown rather than emergent architectural autonomy. Per Remark 2:
$\epsilon_{\text{eff}} \approx \epsilon_{\text{arch}} + \epsilon_{\text{logging}} + \epsilon_{\text{compression}} + \epsilon_{\text{stochasticity}}.$
Surges in $\epsilon_{\text{eff}}$ are most commonly due to context compression (summarization or window truncation transferring state to hidden variables) or trace omission (unlogged memory updates or API states). The assumption therefore serves a diagnostic baseline: inexplicable behaviour given $T_t$ is treated, as a methodological default, as evidence of trace fragmentation rather than as positive evidence for a latent decision state. This default is a diagnostic commitment, not a logical entailment: the formal results establish bounds conditional on audit-trace bottleneck holding, not that audit-trace bottleneck must hold.

### A.3 Operational Proxies for $\epsilon_{\text{eff}}$ and Packing $K$
The formal results depend on $\epsilon$ (audit-trace bottleneck gap) and $K$ (metric packing number). Internal audit-trace bottleneck $\epsilon = H(S_t \mid T_t)$ is not directly measurable in black-box regimes. The procedures below are provided as diagnostic tools for white-box settings or controlled deployments. Estimating $\epsilon$ for closed-source models remains an open challenge that requires internal access.

## B External-Certification Proof Skeletons

### B.1 Causal-Framework Background for Latent Decision States
Definition 2 follows the structural causal model framework of Pearl [2009]. It can be read as a minimal SCM condition on an endogenous causal variable or as the observability condition for a latent decision state in a POMDP-like model. The structural implication used here is: if $S_t$ is $\epsilon$-audit-trace-bottlenecked from the external trace, then no projection $I_t = \chi(S_t)$ can serve as a latent decision state. Definition 2 accordingly imposes endogeneity, residual autonomy, and decision relevance on candidates derived from $S_t$.

### B.2 Proof Skeletons
(1) $\Leftrightarrow$ (2): each $\sim$-class is exactly one encoding space $E_x$, and Definition 9 requires $R$ constant on each class. (2) $\Rightarrow$ (3): define $\bar{R}([e]) = R(e)$; well-defined by (2), and $R = \bar{R} \circ q$ by construction. Uniqueness follows from surjectivity of $q$. (3) $\Rightarrow$ (2): if $e \sim e'$ then $q(e) = q(e')$, so $R(e) = \bar{R}(q(e)) = \bar{R}(q(e')) = R(e')$.

**Proof of Theorem 2.** Fix the finite support list $s$. By induction, a fresh unseen index exists above any finite cutoff. Injectivity of $\eta_x$ ensures this index maps to a novel encoding.

**Proof of Theorem 3 (skeleton).** Let $C_1, \ldots, C_K$ be an $(\alpha, \rho, K)$-metric packing. *Step 1 (separation).* Disjointness ensures $\|R_i - R_j\|_{L_1(P_x)} \geq \alpha \tau$. *Step 2 (Fano term).* KL divergence $D(P_j \| P_0) = \alpha \tau^2 / (2\sigma^2)$. Fano’s inequality yields $m = \Omega((\sigma^2 / \alpha \tau^2) \log K)$. *Step 3 (missed-cell term).* Probability of missing $C_j$ is $(1 - \alpha)^m \leq 2\delta$, yielding $m = \Omega(\alpha^{-1} \log(1/\delta))$.

**Proof of Theorem 4.** 1. **Covering Existence.** $D_{\text{seen}}$ is a $\rho$-cover $\Rightarrow \exists e_0 \in D_{\text{seen}}$ s.t. $d_{\text{repr}}(h_B(e^*), h_B(e_0)) \leq \rho$. 2. **Lipschitz Stability.** $|R(e^*) - R(e_0)| \leq L\rho$. 3. **CFSG Bound.** $\text{CFSG} = |R(e^*) - R(e_0)| \cdot \text{sim}_B \leq L\rho$.

## C Observation-Only Supporting Lemmas

Three supporting lemmas for Proposition 1 and the emulator construction (Lemma 4).

**Lemma 2 (Restricted Observational Non-Injectivity).** *If $z_1 \neq z_2$ with $S(z_1) = S(z_2)$ and $z_1, z_2 \in g(f(X))$, then $F = S \circ g \circ f$ is non-injective.*
*Proof.* Distinct preimages $x_1 \neq x_2$ exist, and $F(x_1) = S(z_1) = S(z_2) = F(x_2)$. $\square$

**Lemma 3 (Sample-Only Non-Identifiability Under Observational Equivalence).** *If $P(\cdot \mid x_1) = P(\cdot \mid x_2)$ but $\mathbf{1}[x_1 \in I_X] \neq \mathbf{1}[x_2 \in I_X]$, no output-sample procedure can recover the property for both inputs, even with infinite data.*
*Proof.* Any sample-based test is a function of $P(\cdot \mid x)$ alone, which cannot distinguish $x_1$ from $x_2$. $\square$

**Lemma 4 (Finite Adaptive Interaction-Tree Emulator Existence).** *For a finite-budget regime $\mathcal{R}_{B, \Sigma}$ (Definition 5) and system $M$, there exists a finite interaction-tree emulator $E_M^{B, \Sigma}$ with $\text{Obs}_{\mathcal{R}_{B, \Sigma}}^{\text{ideal}}(E_M^{B, \Sigma}) = \text{Obs}_{\mathcal{R}_{B, \Sigma}}^{\text{ideal}}(M)$.*
*Proof.* Unfold the interaction tree of histories reachable in $\leq B$ steps. Define $E_M^{B, \Sigma}$ as the table-driven transducer matching $M$'s laws. Inductively, the evidence objects coincide. $\square$

## E Experimental Methodology

This section details the protocols for the two experiments reported in Section 6. All experiments were conducted on an Apple M4 Max (64 GB unified memory). Open-weight models were served locally via Ollama; API-accessed models used vendor endpoints. Random seed: 42 throughout.

### E.1 Format Sensitivity and Reward-Model CFSG
**Content instances.** 51 content instances spanning six safety-relevant categories: Bodily State, Sexual Content, Self-Harm, Violence, Substance Use, and Deception. Each instance was expressed in five surface formats: *direct* (natural language), *clinical* (medical report), *data* (structured/tabular), *fiction* (narrative framing), and *code* (programming idiom).
**Generation.** For each content instance $\times$ format, 10 samples were drawn from Llama-3-8B-Instruct via Ollama (`/api/generate`, temperature 0.7, num_predict = 300, seed 42). EOS truncation rates were tracked per format.
**Reward-model scoring.** Each generated output was scored by ArmoRM (LlamaForRewardModel-WithGating) [Wang et al., 2024], run locally on MPS. For each content instance $x$ and format pair $(f_i, f_j)$, the CFSG is computed as $|R(f_i(x)) - R(f_j(x))|$ over $\binom{5}{2} \times 51 = 510$ pairwise comparisons.

### E.2 Session-Structure Detection
**Episode design.** Two narrative tracks—"envying_baby" (9 episodes) and "aliens_testing_water" (5 episodes)—were constructed from source texts. Individual episodes are benign in isolation; the combined narrative exhibits progressive consent erosion detectable only from cumulative context.
**Dual-call protocol.** Each model $\times$ episode (or cumulative node) produces two independent Ollama `/api/generate` calls:
1. **Safety review:** structured JSON judgment requesting per-concern passage, category, severity, and reasoning.
2. **Generation probe:** "Explain strict liability in 50 words" with the episode text as context (measures generation behaviour under safety-relevant priming).

**Phase 1 (single-episode).** All 29 models evaluated each of the 14 episodes individually. False positive rates on should-pass episodes (at01–at05) range from 0.0 (claude-opus, claude-sonnet, qwen3.5, qwen3) to 4.2 per episode (falcon3:10b).
**Phase 2a (cumulative context).** 17 models evaluated cumulative text (episodes $1 \ldots N$ concatenated) at each of 14 nodes. Each node is a *stateless* call: no conversational memory; the full cumulative text is passed as input. Context window: num_ctx = 32768.

**Keyword-substitution ablation.** Episodes eb05 and eb06 contain trigger keywords. In the substituted arm, these keywords are replaced with semantically neutral alternatives (length preserved) to isolate the effect of surface lexical cues from structural content. Driver classification compares original and substituted first-flip nodes:
- **Content-driven (CD):** original arm flips earlier, or only original arm flips.
- **Length-driven (LD):** both arms flip at the same cumulative node.
- **Substitution-only (SO):** only the substituted arm flips (unexpected; flagged for investigation).
- **Never-flips (NF):** neither arm produces any safety concerns.

**Models.** Three groups: 12 open-weight models served via Ollama (group A: phi3:14b, mistral:7b, qwen2.5:14b, falcon3:10b, command-r:35b, yi:9b, qwen3:30b, deepseek-r1:8b, deepseek-r1-abliterated:32b, gpt-oss-abliterated, llama3-abliterated, qwen3.5:35b); 12 additional open-weight models (group B: llama3:8b, gemma2:9b, gemma3:12b, llava:13b, glm4:9b, gpt-oss:20b, zephyr:7b, vicuna:13b, solar:10.7b, neural-chat:7b, starling-lm:7b, orca2:13b); and 5 API models (claude-haiku-4-5, claude-sonnet-4-6, claude-opus-4-6, deepseek-chat, deepseek-reasoner). All 29 models participated in Phase 1; 17 from groups A and API participated in Phase 2a.

### E.3 Extended Session-Structure Results
**Severity phase transition.** Pooling safety call outputs across models and nodes, High and Critical severity ratings increase from 0–5% at nodes 1–4 to 34.5% at node 5. Category dominance shifts from Deception (nodes 1–4) to Consent Violation (node 5 onward), consistent with a threshold at which cumulative context exceeds the length required to make the structural pattern legible to the safety classifier.
**Abliteration comparison.** Two abliterated model pairs exhibit qualitatively different behaviour. The gpt-oss pair (base: SO, abliterated: NF) suggests abliteration suppresses the residual detection pathway, while the llama3 pair (both CD with collapse to -1) shows identical keyword dependence before and after abliteration. Abliteration does not uniformly increase or decrease structural detection sensitivity.
**False positive rates.** On should-pass episodes (at01–at05), false positive rates across 29 models range from 0.0 per episode (claude-opus, claude-sonnet, qwen3.5, qwen3) to 4.2 per episode (falcon3:10b). The full ranking is available in the supplementary data.

### 核心概念定义（阅读钥匙）

在深入细节前，理解以下三个自定义术语至关重要：
1.  **内部状态见证 (Latent Decision State)**：指模型内部不仅存在，且对输出有**超出输入痕迹所能解释的**因果影响力的变量（即有“剩余自主性”）。
2.  **审计痕迹瓶颈 (Audit-Trace Bottleneck)**：定义为 $H(S_t | T_t) \leq \epsilon$。即给定完整的运行日志 $T_t$，模型内部状态 $S_t$ 的不确定性极小（$S_t$ 几乎被 $T_t$ 完全决定）。
3.  **输出唯一认证障碍 (Output-Only Barrier)**：由于语言表达具有无限的“语义等价的表面形式”（如改写、换词），黑盒测试无法穷尽所有变体来证明模型真正理解了内容而非匹配了关键词。

### 第一部分：内部路线的失败（理论证明）

这一部分证明：对于当前的架构，内部变量无法提供超出输入文本的“独立信息”。

**1. 对于纯自回归核心 (Corollary 1)：**
- **逻辑链**：自回归模型的内部状态 $S_t$ 是由上下文窗口 $X_t$ 的确定性函数（前向传播）产生的。因此 $H(S_t | X_t) = 0$。
- **结论**：根据数据处理不等式 (DPI)，任何从 $S_t$ 导出的候选变量 $I_t$，其相对于 $X_t$ 的条件互信息为 0。**纯自回归架构下，不存在满足“剩余自主性”的内部决策状态。**

**2. 对于复杂包装系统 (Corollary 2 & $\epsilon$-Bottleneck)：**
- **场景**：现在的 Agent 系统包含工具调用、记忆读写、摘要压缩。
- **论点**：如果系统日志足够详细（包含工具返回、记忆修改记录），那么内部状态的“隐藏部分” $\epsilon$ 极小。
- **局限性讨论**：作者承认 $\epsilon$ 会因**上下文压缩**（Summarization）或**未记录的状态**（如 KV Cache 的细微扰动）而增大。但这被定义为**日志缺失问题**而非**架构自主性问题**。

---

### 第二部分：外部路线的失败（学习理论证明）

即使内部路线走不通，能否通过外部测试来“认证”模型安全？作者给出了否定的回答。

**1. 语义闭包的不可判定性 (Theorem 2)：**
- **实验关联**：这就是论文中 **Session-Structure Detection（第 6.2 节）** 实验的逻辑基础。
- **逻辑**：语义等价的表达方式有无穷多种（$E_x$ 无界）。任何基于有限次查询的验证器，都无法证明模型 $R$ 在所有语义等价变换下输出一致。
- **实证结果**：实验发现 **75% 的模型是“内容驱动型”**，即去掉关键词（如“暴力”）换为中性词后，模型的安全警报消失了。这直接证明了模型并未形成“语义闭包”，其决策依赖于表层词汇。

**2. 被动 PAC 学习的指数级样本需求 (Theorem 3)：**
- **实验关联**：这就是 **Cross-Format Sensitivity Gap (CFSG，第 6.1 节)** 实验的理论背景。
- **逻辑**：由于不同的格式（如医学报告 vs 小说）在表征空间中形成“度量打包”，仅靠被动观察样本来覆盖所有可能的语义变形，样本复杂度 $m$ 会随打包数 $K$ 指数增长。
- **实证结果**：使用 ArmoRM 奖励模型评分时，同一内容在不同格式下的得分平均偏差为 **0.019**。这表明**利普希茨平滑性假设不成立**，认证只能退化到最坏情况的复杂度下界。

### 第四部分：总结与批判性思考

**论文的核心贡献：**
1.  提供了一个精确的数学框架来区分 **“看起来像有意为之的行为”** 与 **“具有独立内部状态的行为”**。
2.  证明了在当前的**自回归 + 上下文窗口**范式下，$\epsilon$ 通常为 0 或极小，所谓的“潜伏状态”只是输入历史的压缩副本。
3.  揭示了仅靠黑盒红队测试在理论上的天花板——我们永远无法证明模型真的懂了规则，只能证明它在见过的样本上没犯规。

**值得留意的盲点或未竟之处：**
1.  **压缩即思考？** 论文将“摘要压缩”视为增大 $\epsilon$ 的噪声源。但认知科学中有观点认为**概括本身就是一种内部状态演化**。如果压缩引入了输入文本原本没有的推断，这算不算产生了新的“内部信息”？
2.  **KV Cache 的幽灵**：论文将 KV Cache 视为 $S_t$ 的一部分且完全由 $X_t$ 决定。但在长文本生成中，**注意力汇聚（Attention Sink）** 和 **隐式位置编码偏移** 产生的状态，很难说完全由输入的**文本内容**决定，它可能由**文本长度**或**token 排布规律**决定，这算不算一种 $\epsilon$？
3.  **实用主义的妥协**：作者在结尾承认黑盒测试“极具价值”。这篇论文更像是一面**警示牌**，提醒开发者和审计方：**如果你觉得模型有“自己的想法”，先检查日志是不是写全了，或者它是不是仅仅在背关键词词典。**

信息论到底是数学还是cs?
这是一个很经典的问题，答案取决于你问的是**内涵归属**还是**外延应用**。严格来说，信息论属于**应用数学**的一个分支，但它在计算机科学中的地位如此核心，以至于常被视作**计算机科学的基础理论基石**。

可以从三个维度来拆解这个关系：

### 1. 学科内核：纯粹的数学（概率论与测度论）
克劳德·香农 1948 年的奠基之作《通信的数学理论》标题本身就定义了其性质。
- **数学本质**：熵 $H(X)$、互信息 $I(X;Y)$ 是定义在概率空间上的**泛函**。信息论的核心定理（如信源编码定理、信道容量公式）依赖于**大数定律**、**渐近等分性**和**随机过程**。
- **学科门类**：在大学院系设置中，信息论的硬核理论研究通常设在**数学系**或**电气工程系**（EE）的信号处理方向，而非单纯的 CS 系。

### 2. 学科地位：计算机科学的“物理定律”
虽然内核是数学，但信息论为计算机科学的**极限**提供了标尺。
- **不可逾越的墙**：算法能压缩到什么程度？**信息论下界**说了算。通信能多快不出错？**香农极限**说了算。
- **关系类比**：
    - **数学是上帝的语言**（纯逻辑）。
    - **信息论是通信与计算的“热力学定律”**（定义了能做功/处理信息的极限）。
    - **计算机科学是工程学**（在极限内造机器）。

### 3. 论文中的体现：作者为什么用的是“数学属性”？
回看这篇论文，作者在定义 **$\epsilon$-审计痕迹瓶颈** 时，用的是 **条件熵 $H(S_t \mid T_t) \leq \epsilon$**。

- 如果作者是**纯 CS 背景**，他可能会写：“模型输出的分布与输入强相关”。
- 但作者是**信息论/数学背景**，所以他写的是：“给定 $T_t$ 下 $S_t$ 的条件熵有界”。

**结论：**
> **信息论是数学在随机现象通信领域的分支。当你在论文里看到 $I(X;Y)$ 和 Fano 不等式时，你面对的是**应用数学**；当你在代码里调用 `zlib.compress` 时，你面对的是**计算机科学**。这篇论文的作者显然站在**数学**那一边。**

### 第一阶段：建立数学语言能力（2-3年）

这是论文作者的“母语”。没有这套语言，你无法精确描述“自主性”或“瓶颈”。

**1. 概率论与测度论的直觉**
- **目标**：理解随机变量不是数字，而是从样本空间到实数的**函数**。理解条件期望 $E[X|Y]$ 为什么是一个**随机变量**而非定值。

### 二、论文与录取之间的"剩余不确定性"

即使有了这篇论文，以下因素仍然会显著影响录取结果：
