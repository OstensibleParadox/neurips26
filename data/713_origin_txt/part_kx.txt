你要做的是**用Schrems II当类比**，证明你的论点：**规则（GDPR）+ 形式合规（DPC的“调查”）≠ 实质执行**

### 一句话版本（放进论文）：
> The Irish DPC followed every procedural rule in investigating Meta's EU-US data transfers—it opened inquiries, issued draft decisions, maintained documentation—yet its rule-following was performative, producing no enforcement until the EDPB intervened under Article 65, overriding the DPC and imposing a €1.2 billion fine. Procedural compliance without substantive judgment is the regulatory analogue of performative alignment.

### 脚注版本（如果你怕 reviewers 不知道细节）：
> For the full procedural saga, see Case C-311/18 (Schrems II) and EDPB Binding Decision 1/2023. The DPC's formal compliance with GDPR Article 60(3)–(4) (consistency mechanism) produced a draft decision that found Meta in breach of Article 46(1) yet proposed no fine; the EDPB's override under Article 65(1)(a) was necessary to convert procedural compliance into substantive enforcement.

## 💡 核心论点翻译（写给reviewers看）：

**DPC did everything the rules required. It followed the procedure. It produced documentation. It consulted other authorities. And none of it mattered—because rules don't enforce themselves. The EDPB had to step in and exercise judgment: looking at the same facts, applying the same rules, reaching a different conclusion. That's the difference between compliance and accountability.**

## 🎉 你现在只需要这个

别试图解释清楚Schrems II，你只需要：
1. **陈述事实**（DPC走完所有程序但没罚款 → EDPB介入 → €1.2亿罚款）
2. **点出类比**（形式合规 vs 实质执行）
3. **论证论点**（规则需要 judgment 才能生效）
