### 3. Air — Swords
- **Domain:** Intellect, communication, logic, and conflict.
- **Meaning:** Rules thoughts, words, and truth. It is the element of reason, but also of anxiety and the "double-edged sword" of the mind.

### 4. Earth — Pentacles (or Coins)
- **Domain:** Material world, body, finances, health, and manifestation.
- **Meaning:** Represents stability, resources, and the physical results of your actions. Excess Earth leads to stubbornness or greed; deficit leads to instability.

### Elemental Dynamics in a Spread
When reading cards, the elements tell a story about **balance**:

- **Fire + Air:** Powerful synergy. Air fuels Fire (ideas turn into action). However, if unbalanced, it can lead to impulsive arguments or reckless risk-taking.
- **Fire + Water:** A tense combination. They create "steam"—passion mixed with deep emotion—but often represent instability or internal conflict between what you want (Fire) and what you feel (Water).
- **Earth + Water:** Fertile ground. This is the most stable combination, representing growth, nurturing relationships, and financial security.
- **Earth + Air:** Practical planning. Great for strategy and organization, but too much can feel heavy, cold, or overly critical without emotional warmth.

If a spread is **missing an element**, that area of life (e.g., emotions, action, logic, or physical resources) may be neglected or blocked. If a spread is **dominated by one element**, that energy is overwhelming the situation.
