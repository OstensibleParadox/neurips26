# 关于在LaTeX中添加Endmatter部分的建议

## 简短回答

**可以，而且非常适合！** 在LaTeX中创建一个专门的"Endmatter"部分来放置AI工具使用声明是很好的做法。以下是具体实现方案。

---

## 方案一：使用 `\section*` 创建单独的声明章节

在 `\section{Conclusion}` 之后、`\printbibliography` 之前添加：

```latex
\section{Conclusion}
% ... 您的结论内容 ...

% ---------- Endmatter ----------
\section*{Declarations}

\subsection*{Use of AI Tools}
During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted tools solely for LaTeX compilation, debugging, and technical formatting. These tools were not used for generating or shaping the intellectual content, arguments, or analysis presented in this paper. The authors have reviewed and approved the final manuscript and take full responsibility for its content.

\subsection*{Data Availability}
Not applicable. This manuscript does not contain original empirical data.

\subsection*{Competing Interests}
The authors declare no competing interests.

% 如果有其他声明可以继续添加
% \subsection*{Funding}
% ...

\printbibliography
\end{document}
```

**效果**：
- 在结论之后、参考文献之前，会出现一个"Declarations"章节
- 包含AI工具使用、数据可用性、竞争利益等所有声明
- 使用 `\section*` 避免编号（不打断论文的编号体系）

---

## 方案二：使用 `\appendix` 创建附录（如果声明较多）

如果声明内容较多，可以使用附录：

```latex
\section{Conclusion}
% ... 您的结论内容 ...

\appendix
\section{Declarations and Supplementary Information}

\subsection{Use of AI Tools}
During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted tools solely for LaTeX compilation, debugging, and technical formatting. These tools were not used for generating or shaping the intellectual content, arguments, or analysis presented in this paper. The authors have reviewed and approved the final manuscript and take full responsibility for its content.

\subsection{Data Availability Statement}
This theoretical paper does not involve original empirical data. All sources cited are publicly available as indicated in the references.

\subsection{Competing Interests}
The authors declare that they have no competing financial or non-financial interests that could have appeared to influence the work reported in this paper.

\printbibliography
\end{document}
```

**效果**：
- 声明部分会获得附录编号（如 "Appendix A: Declarations..."）
- 适合有多项声明需要详细说明的情况

---

## 方案三：创建一个专门的命令（最优雅的LaTeX风格）

在导言区定义一个命令：

```latex
% 在导言区添加
\newcommand{\declarations}{%
  \vspace{2ex}
  \noindent\textbf{Declarations}
  
  \smallskip
  \noindent\textbf{Use of AI Tools:} During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted tools solely for LaTeX compilation, debugging, and technical formatting. These tools were not used for generating or shaping the intellectual content. The authors take full responsibility for the publication.
  
  \smallskip
  \noindent\textbf{Competing Interests:} The authors declare no competing interests.
  
  \smallskip
  \noindent\textbf{Data Availability:} Not applicable.
  \vspace{2ex}
}
```

然后在正文中使用：

```latex
\section{Conclusion}
% ... 您的结论内容 ...

\declarations

\printbibliography
\end{document}
```

**效果**：
- 代码更整洁
- 易于统一管理所有声明
- 可以轻松调整格式

---

## 方案四：创建单独的 `declarations.tex` 文件（最模块化）

创建一个新文件 `declarations.tex`：

```latex
% declarations.tex
\section*{Declarations}

\subsection*{Use of AI Tools}
During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted tools solely for LaTeX compilation, debugging, and technical formatting. These tools were not used for generating or shaping the intellectual content, arguments, or analysis presented in this paper. The authors have reviewed and approved the final manuscript and take full responsibility for its content.

\subsection*{Data Availability Statement}
This theoretical paper does not involve original empirical data. All sources cited are publicly available as indicated in the references.

\subsection*{Competing Interests}
The authors declare that they have no competing financial or non-financial interests that could have appeared to influence the work reported in this paper.

\subsection*{Ethical Approval}
Not applicable.

\subsection*{Informed Consent}
Not applicable.
```

然后在主文件中 `\input` 它：

```latex
\section{Conclusion}
% ... 您的结论内容 ...

\input{declarations}

\printbibliography
\end{document}
```

**效果**：
- 最模块化的组织方式
- 主文件保持简洁
- 声明内容独立管理
- 可以用于多个投稿（只需修改一处）

---

## 推荐方案

**对于您的论文，我推荐方案一或方案二**，因为：

1. **期刊预期**：伦理学期刊通常接受在文末有独立的声明章节
2. **完整性**：可以一次性包含所有需要的声明（AI工具、竞争利益、数据可用性等）
3. **清晰性**：审稿人一眼就能看到所有合规声明
4. **不干扰正文**：声明在结论之后，不影响论证的连贯性

## 二、需要立即修改的内容

### 2.1 移除所有声明（包括竞争利益声明）

**问题**：您计划在正文末尾添加的 `Declarations` 章节，根据 Springer Nature 指南，**必须完全移除**。

**解决方案**：**不要**在正文中添加任何声明章节。所有声明（AI工具使用、竞争利益、数据可用性等）都必须在投稿系统（Snapp）中填写。

**具体操作**：
- 删除您计划添加的 `\section*{Declarations}` 及相关内容
- **不要**在正文中提及任何声明信息
- 将所有声明信息准备好，在投稿时在系统界面填写

### 2.2 检查自我引用格式

**问题**：指南要求使用第三人称引用自己已发表的作品。

**检查您的论文中是否有类似表述**：

| 原文 | 是否合规 | 修改建议 |
|------|----------|----------|
| `(Zhang, 2024)` | ✅ 合规（第三人称引用） | 无需修改 |
| `as we have previously argued (Zhang, 2024)` | ❌ 不合规（"we"暴露身份） | 改为 `as has been previously argued (Zhang, 2024)` |
| `our earlier work (Zhao et al., 2023)` | ❌ 不合规（"our"暴露身份） | 改为 `earlier work (Zhao et al., 2023)` |
| `this paper extends the analysis in Zhang (2025)` | ✅ 合规（第三人称） | 无需修改 |

**核心原则**：只要不出现 **"we"、"our"、"the authors"** 等指向当前作者的词，直接引用自己的已发表论文是允许的。
