# 关于AI工具使用声明的建议

## 简短回答

**可以，但需要调整表述**。您的想法方向正确，但目前的表述方式不符合学术规范。以下是具体分析和修改建议。

---

## 为什么需要调整？

您目前的表述存在两个问题：

1. **Claude Code** 不是标准学术引用名称（应使用更通用的描述）
2. **"通过git提交证明原创性"** 不属于AI工具使用声明的范畴，且git提交本身不直接证明原创性（只证明时间线）

期刊要求的是**透明披露**，而非**证明原创性**。

---

## 修改建议

### 方案一：最简洁的合规版本（推荐）

在论文的**适当位置**（见下文）添加：

> During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted coding tools for LaTeX compilation and debugging. After using these tools, the authors reviewed and edited the content as needed and take full responsibility for the content of the publication.

### 方案二：更详细的版本（如果您希望更透明）

> During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted tools (including Claude Code) for technical tasks such as LaTeX compilation, debugging, and code formatting. These tools were not used for generating academic content, arguments, or analysis. All intellectual contributions, including the conceptual framework, philosophical analysis, legal arguments, and conclusions, are the authors' own work. The authors have reviewed and approved the final manuscript and take full responsibility for its content.

### 方案三：区分"内容生成"与"技术辅助"的版本

> **Use of AI Tools**
> 
> The authors declare that no AI tools were used for generating the intellectual content, arguments, or analysis presented in this paper. AI-assisted coding tools were used solely for technical purposes related to LaTeX manuscript preparation, including compilation, debugging, and formatting. The authors maintain full responsibility for the scholarly content and take accountability for all arguments and conclusions.

## 放在论文的哪个位置？

根据期刊指南："should be properly documented in the Methods section (and if a Methods section is not available, in a suitable alternative part) of the manuscript."

您的论文没有Methods章节，建议放在：

### 选项A：正文第一个注释（推荐）

在引言末尾或标题下方添加一个脚注：

```latex
\footnote{During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted tools solely for LaTeX compilation and debugging. The authors reviewed and edited all content and take full responsibility for the publication.}
```

### 选项B：正文末尾、参考文献之前

在 `\section{Conclusion}` 之后、`\printbibliography` 之前，添加一个单独的章节：

```latex
\section*{Declaration of AI Use}
During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted coding tools exclusively for technical tasks related to LaTeX manuscript preparation (compilation, debugging, and formatting). ### 选项C：Title Page（如果您愿意）
```

## 关于"git提交证明原创性"

您提到的"通过在git上提交commit的方式证明论文原创性"：

1. **这与AI工具使用声明是两个不同的问题**
2. 如果您想保留git历史作为原创性证据，**不需要在论文中声明**，这是您个人的记录保存方式
3. 投稿时期刊不会要求查看git历史

## 最终推荐版本

综合以上分析，我建议使用以下简洁且合规的声明，放在正文第一个脚注：

```latex
\footnote{During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted tools solely for LaTeX compilation and debugging. The authors reviewed and edited all content and take full responsibility for the publication.}
```

- 明确说明用途（LaTeX编译和调试）

# 关于在LaTeX中添加Endmatter部分的建议

## 简短回答

**可以，而且非常适合！** 在LaTeX中创建一个专门的"Endmatter"部分来放置AI工具使用声明是很好的做法。以下是具体实现方案。

---

## 方案一：使用 `\section*` 创建单独的声明章节

在 `\section{Conclusion}` 之后、`\printbibliography` 之前添加：

```latex
\section{Conclusion}
% ... 您的结论内容 ...

% ---------- Endmatter ----------
\section*{Declarations}

\subsection*{Use of AI Tools}
During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted tools solely for LaTeX compilation, debugging, and technical formatting. These tools were not used for generating or shaping the intellectual content, arguments, or analysis presented in this paper. The authors have reviewed and approved the final manuscript and take full responsibility for its content.

\subsection*{Data Availability}
Not applicable. This manuscript does not contain original empirical data.

\subsection*{Competing Interests}
The authors declare no competing interests.

% 如果有其他声明可以继续添加
% \subsection*{Funding}
% ...

\printbibliography
\end{document}
```

**效果**：
- 在结论之后、参考文献之前，会出现一个"Declarations"章节
- 包含AI工具使用、数据可用性、竞争利益等所有声明
- 使用 `\section*` 避免编号（不打断论文的编号体系）

---

## 方案二：使用 `\appendix` 创建附录（如果声明较多）

如果声明内容较多，可以使用附录：

```latex
\section{Conclusion}
% ... 您的结论内容 ...

\appendix
\section{Declarations and Supplementary Information}

\subsection{Use of AI Tools}
During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted tools solely for LaTeX compilation, debugging, and technical formatting. These tools were not used for generating or shaping the intellectual content, arguments, or analysis presented in this paper. The authors have reviewed and approved the final manuscript and take full responsibility for its content.

\subsection{Data Availability Statement}
This theoretical paper does not involve original empirical data. All sources cited are publicly available as indicated in the references.

\subsection{Competing Interests}
The authors declare that they have no competing financial or non-financial interests that could have appeared to influence the work reported in this paper.

\printbibliography
\end{document}
```

**效果**：
- 声明部分会获得附录编号（如 "Appendix A: Declarations..."）
- 适合有多项声明需要详细说明的情况

---

## 方案三：创建一个专门的命令（最优雅的LaTeX风格）

在导言区定义一个命令：

```latex
% 在导言区添加
\newcommand{\declarations}{%
  \vspace{2ex}
  \noindent\textbf{Declarations}
  
  \smallskip
  \noindent\textbf{Use of AI Tools:} During the preparation of this work, the authors used AI-assisted tools solely for LaTeX compilation, debugging, and technical formatting. These tools were not used for generating or shaping the intellectual content. The authors take full responsibility for the publication.
  
  \smallskip
  \noindent\textbf{Competing Interests:} The authors declare no competing interests.
  
  \smallskip
  \noindent\textbf{Data Availability:} Not applicable.
  \vspace{2ex}
}
```

然后在正文中使用：

```latex
\section{Conclusion}
% ... 您的结论内容 ...

\declarations

\printbibliography
\end{document}
```

**效果**：
- 代码更整洁
