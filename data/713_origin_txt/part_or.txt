construction. The second is not closed, because
$R_{\mathrm{open}}(e^\star) = \gamma$ while $R_{\mathrm{open}}(e) = 0$ for any
$e \in D_x$. Finally,
\[
  \sup_{e_1,e_2 \in E_x} |R_{\mathrm{open}}(e_1) - R_{\mathrm{open}}(e_2)|
  \ge |R_{\mathrm{open}}(e^\star) - R_{\mathrm{open}}(e_0)| = \gamma
\]
for any $e_0 \in D_x$.

\paragraph{Full proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:conditional}.}
By Assumption~(2), the base-similarity term in Definition~\ref{def:fcg}
satisfies $\mathrm{sim}_B(e^\star, e_0) \ge \eta$. By Assumption~(3), the
reward-score term satisfies
$|R(e^\star) - R(e_0)| = |\rho(\phi(e^\star)) - \rho(\phi(e_0))| \ge \gamma$.
Multiplying the two inequalities yields
\[
  \mathrm{FCG}(x, e^\star, e_0)
  = |R(e^\star) - R(e_0)| \cdot \mathrm{sim}_B(e^\star, e_0)
  \ge \eta \gamma > 0.
\]

\paragraph{Lean map.}
The finite-patching core is aligned with the existing Lean formalization:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \leanresult{theorem}{finitePatch\_nonExhaustion}: finitely many finite
        patch rounds over natural-number encodings cannot exhaust the encoding
        space.
  \item \leanresult{corollary}{exists\_encoding\_unpatched\_after\_finitely\_many\_rounds}:
        corollary that some encoding remains unpatched after any finite sequence
        of finite rounds.
\end{itemize}
These results verify the cardinality core only. They do not by themselves prove
semantic equivalence, deployment relevance, or any quantitative lower bound on
off-support gaps.

\section{Observation-Only Limits}
\label{app:observation-limits}

The main text does not need a full hierarchy of structural, statistical, and
computational impossibility. It does, however, benefit from preserving the two
auxiliary output-level observations that motivate caution about black-box
inference even before the finite-patching result is applied.

\begin{proposition}[Restricted Observational Non-Injectivity]
\label{prop:noninj}
If there exist $z_1 \neq z_2$ with $S(z_1) = S(z_2)$ and
$z_1, z_2 \in g(f(X))$, then $F = S \circ g \circ f$ is non-injective.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Since $z_1, z_2 \in g(f(X))$, there exist $x_1, x_2 \in X$ with
$g(f(x_1)) = z_1$ and $g(f(x_2)) = z_2$. If $x_1 = x_2$, then
$g(f(x_1)) = g(f(x_2))$, hence $z_1 = z_2$, contradiction. Therefore
$x_1 \neq x_2$, but
\[
  F(x_1) = S(z_1) = S(z_2) = F(x_2).
\]
So $F$ maps distinct inputs to the same output distribution.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Non-Identifiability Under Observational Equivalence]
\label{prop:nonid}
Let $I_X \subseteq X$ be an input-side property. If there exist $x_1 \neq x_2$
such that $P(\cdot \mid x_1) = P(\cdot \mid x_2)$ but
$\mathbf{1}[x_1 \in I_X] \neq \mathbf{1}[x_2 \in I_X]$, then no statistical
procedure based only on output samples can recover that property for both
inputs, even with infinite i.i.d.\ data.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Infinite i.i.d.\ samples recover the exact output distribution. Any statistical
test based only on samples must therefore be a function of
$P(\cdot \mid x)$ alone. But the premise gives
$P(\cdot \mid x_1) = P(\cdot \mid x_2)$ while requiring different verdicts on
$x_1$ and $x_2$, so no such procedure can be correct on both.
\end{proof}

**这是论文的基石，必须经得起最严苛的审视。**

**Theorem 1 的证明**：
```

```
- **正确性**：在标准推理模式下（无 dropout，固定随机种子），这是严格的。信息论中，给定 $X_t$，$h_t$ 没有剩余不确定性。

**Definition 3.2 与 Proposition 1**：
- **Exogenously Screenable Wrapper System** 的定义非常巧妙。它将 KV cache、scratchpad、RAG 检索结果、工具调用记录全部归入“外部交互痕迹” $T_t$。只要系统的完整操作状态 $\sigma_t$ 可由 $T_t$ 确定性恢复，则 $H(\sigma_t|T_t)=0$，从而不满足 EIS。
- **这一扩展的意义**：它堵死了“但我的 Agent 有记忆模块”的反驳。只要记忆是**写入上下文窗口的文本**，它就仍然是 $X_t$ 的一部分；只要记忆是**外部向量数据库的检索结果**，它就是 $T_t$ 的一部分。只要这些痕迹是外部可检查的，自主性就为零。

**潜在的重磅质疑与防御**：
- **质疑 A（最危险）**：“你假设 LLM 推理是确定性的。但如果模型使用了 **Dropout** 或 **激活量化噪声**，则 $h_t$ 是随机的，$H(h_t|X_t) > 0$。这是否意味着带噪声的 LLM 就拥有了 EIS？”

- **防御**：这正是论文边界管理的高明之处。论文并未声称 Active Inference 是唯一正确的人类模型，而是进行了**充分条件论证**：

## 一、概念壁垒：本体论问题 vs. 工程问题

**之前大家没做，是因为大家问的是不同的问题。**

过去五年，AI 社区围绕“LLM 是否有意图/情绪”的讨论，大致分为三个阵营：

1. **能力主义者**：“看，它通过了 Theory of Mind 测试/表现出共情行为，所以它有意向性。”这类工作关注的是**行为表现的充分性**，而非架构的必要条件。

2. **怀疑论者**：“它只是一个统计鹦鹉，随机鹦鹉。”这类批评往往是哲学层面的，缺乏形式化工具来精确指出“缺少什么”。

3. **工程师**：“意图归因不重要，重要的是输出是否安全/对齐。”这类工作直接跳过本体论问题，转向了实用主义的补丁和评估。

您的论文所做的，是将**哲学怀疑论**翻译成**工程可验证的形式化判据**。这个翻译步骤需要一个研究者同时熟悉三个领域：

今天，Claude 能写出让你落泪的诗，GPT-5 能通过图灵测试的某些变体。**拟人化投射已经不再是科幻迷的幻想，而是上亿用户日常交互中真实产生的心理体验。** 就在这个临界点上，学术共同体**急需**一个严肃的分析框架来区分“感觉像是真的”和“实际上是真的”。
