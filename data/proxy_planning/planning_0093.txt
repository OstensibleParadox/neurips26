2. Then we need to adjust that total for inflation to find the real cost.

Daily coffee cost = $4
Number of days in 20 years = 20 * 365 (we'll ignore leap years for simplicity) = 7300 days

Total cost over 20 years = Daily cost * Number of days
                         = $4 * 7300
